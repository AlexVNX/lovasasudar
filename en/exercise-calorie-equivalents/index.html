The transparent formula

kcal/min = MET × 3.5 × weight in kilograms ÷ 200. Pounds are converted to kilograms before calculating. Food energy is then divided by the estimated kcal per minute.

Why it is not exact

Intensity

The same activity name can hide very different real effort.

Individual variation

Fitness, technique, terrain and physiology are not captured.

No gender multiplier

We do not apply a fixed sex or gender adjustment that would imply false precision.
