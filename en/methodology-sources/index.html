How the numbers are cooked

Methodology, sources and entertainment disclaimer

Market-specific data, visible servings and an unapologetically large asterisk.

LoVasASudar is an entertainment website. It provides approximate recreational comparisons only. It is not a medical, nutrition, fitness, diet, eating-disorder, alcohol-safety or weight-loss service and does not recommend compensating for food with exercise.

Catalog rules

Market first

English pages use United States sources. Spanish pages use Spain sources. A US figure is never presented as a Spanish product value.

Serving required

Every record stores product, serving, calories or range, source, URL, market and checked date.

Estimate visible

Without an applicable official brand source, the brand is removed and a generic range is labeled as an estimate.

Exercise calculation

kcal/min = MET × 3.5 × weight (kg) ÷ 200. Results are approximate because the MET is a reference and real intensity varies. No fixed gender adjustment is applied.

Google crawl and indexing controls

  • Every Spanish and English page has a self-referencing canonical.
  • Each language pair has reciprocal hreflang links plus x-default.
  • All indexable URLs are listed in the XML sitemap and allowed by robots.txt.
  • Pages return real HTML without JavaScript redirects and include Article or FAQ structured data where appropriate.
  • Final inclusion in Google’s index is controlled by Google and must be confirmed in Google Search Console’s URL Inspection report.

Entertainment, not treatment

Calories and activity minutes are used as a humorous translation between two rough quantities. They do not describe an individual’s health, prescribe behavior, diagnose anything or imply that eating requires compensation. Anyone making health decisions should consult an appropriately qualified professional.
