Alcohol matters

ABV and pour size drive much of the result. Two drinks with the same name can differ when one has a heavier pour.

Mixers add up

Soda, juice and syrup widen the range. A zero-sugar mixer changes the mixer, not the spirit.

US units

Brand values use official 12 fl oz servings. Wine uses 5 fl oz and cocktail ranges state their assumed pour.

FAQ

Does “light” mean zero calories?

No. It is a product description, not a zero-calorie claim. Check the exact serving and official label.

Why are cocktails ranges?

Because recipes and pours are not standardized. A range is more honest than a made-up exact number.
